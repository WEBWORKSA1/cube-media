About

Six faces. One mission.

Make the creator economy easier to understand, easier to price and easier to earn a living in.

Cube.Media is an independent publication and toolkit for the creator economy. We built it because the information creators need most (what content is worth, what brands pay, how platforms really reward you) is scattered across paywalls, sales pages and guesswork.

What we believe

  • Free tools, honest ranges. Every calculator shows ranges and assumptions, not flattering single numbers.
  • Creators are businesses. We cover revenue, pricing and operations, not just "going viral".
  • Fair pay for talent. Our talent network never charges creatives a fee to apply.
  • Clear labels. Sponsored content, affiliate links and ads are always disclosed.

How we make money

We keep the lights on with clearly labelled advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, paid job listings, growth services and supporter donations. Editorial decisions are never sold. Read our disclosures.
