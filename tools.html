Tool FAQ

How accurate is the YouTube money calculator?

It uses creator RPM ranges typical for each niche, adjusted for audience geography. Your real RPM depends on seasonality (Q4 is highest), watch time, ad formats and viewer location. Use the range for planning, not as a promise.

What's the difference between CPM and RPM?

CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions. RPM is what you earn per 1,000 views, after YouTube's share and across all views, including ones that showed no ad. RPM is always lower than CPM.

What is a good engagement rate?

It depends on audience size. Accounts under 5k followers often see 3–5%. Accounts with 100k+ followers average around 1%. Our calculator grades you against the benchmark for your tier.

How should I price my first sponsorship?

Start from your average views (not subscribers), apply a niche CPM of $15–$35 for a YouTube integration, then adjust for engagement and usage rights. Our sponsorship pricing guide walks through it step by step.

Do you store what I type?

No. Every calculation runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you submit a form.
