Privacy Policy

Effective: September 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Cube.Media ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, and how we use it.

1. Information you give us

When you submit a form (growth plan, talent brief, application, contest entry, pledge, advertising inquiry, newsletter or contact), we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, company and message. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond to you, provide the service you asked for, and send the newsletter if you subscribed.

2. Information collected automatically

All calculators run in your browser, and their inputs are not transmitted. If you accept cookies, we may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage (pages visited, device type, approximate location). We store your theme and cookie choice in your browser's local storage.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

We use or may use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more at how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

4. Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you click play. After that, YouTube's privacy policy applies. Links to payment providers (PayPal, Stripe, Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors) take you to their sites, where their policies apply. We never see or store your card details.

5. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent at any time. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us by email or use the contact form.

6. Retention & security

We keep inquiry data only as long as needed to respond and for legitimate business records, typically no more than 24 months. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

7. Children

This site is intended for a general audience aged 13+. Contest entrants under 18 need parental consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date above when we do.
